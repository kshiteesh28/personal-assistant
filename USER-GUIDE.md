# 📖 Flow-State User Guide

Welcome to Flow-State. This guide will help you understand the core concepts and advanced features to optimize your productivity.

## 1. The Daily Brief
The first thing you see every day is your **Daily Brief**. 
- **Today's Schedule**: A timeline merge of your meetings and tasks.
- **Tasks To Do**: Your immediate priority list.
- **Controls**: Use the **Start** button on a task to begin. Flow-State will highlight your active task with a pulse effect.

## 2. Managing Tasks
Tasks in Flow-State have "smart" properties:
- **Energy Level**: Assign High/Medium/Low energy to tasks. The scheduler will try to put High Energy tasks in your peak morning hours.
- **Pinned Time**: If a task *must* happen at 2:00 PM, use the Pinned Time picker.
- **Splittable**: Long tasks (e.g., 4 hours) can be marked as splittable, allowing Flow-State to break them into smaller blocks around your meetings.

## 3. The Chat Panel
The chat (accessible via the `💬` button or `Ctrl+K`) is the fastest way to interact.
- **Slash Commands**: Type `/` to see available commands like `/task`, `/note`, `/remind`, or `/brief`.
- **Interactive Suggestions**: When creating a task, hover over the parameter chips (Priority, Energy, etc.) to quickly add metrics without typing.
- **Natural Language**: You can also just talk to the AI (type or use voice) to ask questions about your day.

## 4. Downtime Pipeline
Finished all your work early? A meeting got cancelled? 
- Go to the **Downtime** tab.
- Add "backburner" items like "Read research paper" or "Clean inbox".
- When Flow-State detects a gap in your schedule, it will proactively suggest an item from this pipeline.

## 5. Proactive Nudges
Flow-State cares about your health. It will periodically send "Nudges":
- **Wellness**: Reminders to hydrate or check your posture.
- **Break**: Suggestions to walk away from the screen after long focus blocks.
- **Motivation**: Timely encouragement when you're in the middle of deep work.

*Note: You can adjust the frequency (intervals) for each of these in the **Settings** tab.*

## 6. Keyboard Shortcuts
- `Ctrl + K`: Toggle Chat Panel.
- `Ctrl + N`: Quickly open New Task modal.
- `/`: Focus chat and start a command (if not already in an input).
- `Esc`: Close modals or chat.

## 7. Voice Interaction
If enabled in Settings, Flow-State can listen for its wake-word ("Flow-State").
- **Always On**: Just say "Flow-State, add a task to buy milk" at any time.
- **TTS**: Flow-State can read your daily brief or replies aloud to you.

---

### Tips for Success
1. **Be Honest with Estimates**: The scheduler works best when your time estimates are realistic.
2. **Use Buffers**: Meetings automatically get pre-meeting (10m) and post-meeting (5m) buffers. Use these for prep and transition.
3. **Check Settings**: Customize your "Work Hours" so Flow-State knows when you're "on the clock" and when to leave you alone.
